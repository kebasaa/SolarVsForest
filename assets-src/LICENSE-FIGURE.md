# Figure 1 artwork

`fig1-art.png` is adapted from Fig. 1 of Stern R., Muller J. D., Rotenberg E., Amer M., Segev L., Yakir D. (2023),
"Photovoltaic fields largely outperform afforestation efficiency in global climate change mitigation strategies",
PNAS Nexus 2(11):pgad352, https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgad352.

Changes: artwork redrawn without text on a transparent background, guide lines recoloured for a dark page; the
website overlays the values as interactive labels.
